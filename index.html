    My Disaster Movie Review: Exit

    I saw the movie Exit. The movie came out in 2019, and it was directed by Lee Sang-geun. It is about a young man who escapes from a city full of toxic gas with his friend. I liked the scene where Yong-nam climbed a tall building because it was very exciting. However, I didn't like some scenes in the middle because they were a little too long. From the movie, I learned that we should stay calm in a disaster. I also learned that we should help each other.
